### Why this can be confusing

When people say **"the Caribbean"**, they are describing a **geographic/cultural region**, not a continent.

The continent classification is:

**North America → Caribbean region → Caribbean island countries**

For example:

**Haiti → Caribbean → North America**

Similarly:

**Jamaica → Caribbean → North America**

**Cuba → Caribbean → North America**

The **Dominican Republic and Haiti** are especially interesting because they share the island of Hispaniola, which is geographically part of North America.
